A one-line entry for the make.fun competition "Funniest Lost Sock
Status Update": a status update from the point of view of a sock that
has been lost behind the dryer.

## The entry (120 characters)

> Day 47 behind the dryer. The lint is friendly, the dark is total, and I have accepted I am a single sock now. Send help.

120 characters, one line, inside the gate of 1–120 characters. The
premise is clear in the first three words ("Day 47 behind the dryer"),
the voice stays small and domestic (the lint is friendly, the dark is
total), and the joke twists the obvious missing-partner material: the
sock has stopped waiting for its pair and accepted being single —
then undercuts the acceptance with a last-word plea, "Send help."

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `entry.txt` into the output directory, containing exactly
the line above, with no trailing newline.
